DevFests are community-led developer events hosted by Google Developer Groups around the globe. From technical deep dives to community networking, here is what is in store for you:

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about attending our GDG event.

Is this event free to attend?
Yes! All Google Developer Groups community events are 100% free of charge. However, because venue seating is limited, you must register in advance to receive your attendee badge.
Who should attend this GDG event?
Everyone is welcome! Whether you are a student, hobbyist developer, senior cloud architect, product designer, or entrepreneur, our talks and workshops are designed with tracks for all experience levels.
What should I bring with me?
For in-person attendees, please bring your digital confirmation pass (or photo ID) and your laptop if you plan to participate in the hands-on afternoon codelabs. Power strips and Wi-Fi credentials will be provided.
How will I access the online livestream?
If you select "Virtual Livestream" upon registering, the direct private YouTube and Google Meet broadcast links will be sent directly to your registered email address 24 hours prior to the event.
